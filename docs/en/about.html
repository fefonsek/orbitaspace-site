About

The person behind Orbita Space: Felipe Fonseca, statistician (UFOP) and master’s student in Administration (IFMG). Experience with Power BI, DAX, R, Python and KPI governance.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Statistics from the Federal University of Ouro Preto (UFOP) and I am a student in the Professional Master’s Program in Administration at the Federal Institute of Minas Gerais (IFMG). I work with data because I like the moment when a number stops being just a number and starts guiding a decision.

Experience

I was a Data Analyst at Prumo Engenharia, where I built Power BI dashboards and DAX and M models, and set up the company’s KPI governance. Some of the work from that period:

  • a working hours monitoring dashboard, with hours worked and overtime alerts;
  • legal department KPIs, such as litigation volume and success rate, with data received through an API;
  • monitoring of traffic violations by fleet drivers;
  • personal protective equipment (PPE) inventory;
  • migration of the company’s BI platform;
  • analytical reports in Quarto and R, such as an employee retention analysis and an audit of benefit payments;
  • KPI curation and governance.

Tools

Power BI, DAX, Power Query, Python, R, Excel and Quarto.

Languages

Native Portuguese, fluent Spanish, advanced English.

Where I am

I live in Formiga, Minas Gerais, Brazil, and work remotely with clients in Brazil and abroad.
